Repeal the Law, Void the Petition

H.B. 75 (2020) wrote into law that a referendum petition is void if the Legislature repeals the law it challenges, and added a temporary stay of a challenged law.

Posted Oct. 19, 2026 · Updated Oct. 19, 2026

Part of Ten Years of Petition Laws, 2016–2026: 35 changes to Utah's initiative and referendum rules, every one written and passed by the Legislature. H.B. 75 (2020) is one of them.

Short version

  • H.B. 75 (2020), primary sponsor Rep. Norman K. Thurston, provides that "a referendum petition is void if the Legislature repeals the proposed law" (H.B. 75 (2020) enrolled text; H.B. 75 (2020) bill status page).
  • It also lets the Lt. Governor temporarily stay a challenged law from taking effect once enough signatures are certified during the count (H.B. 75 (2020) enrolled text).
  • It changed what is posted about signers (voter ID number instead of precinct) and moved several statewide referendum deadlines (H.B. 75 (2020) enrolled text).
  • The bill as introduced in January was only about what is posted about signers. The stay and the void rule were added in the 1st Substitute on March 5, 2020 (H.B. 75 (2020) as introduced; H.B. 75 (2020) 1st Substitute (Thurston); H.B. 75 (2020) bill status page).
  • It passed the House 71–0–4 and the Senate 27–0–2, and was signed March 28, 2020 (House roll call, March 9, 2020; Senate roll call, March 12, 2020; H.B. 75 (2020) bill status page).

What the bill says

Quoted from the enrolled bill (H.B. 75 (2020) enrolled text). Words in [brackets] are struck by the bill.

Utah Code 20A-7-311, lines 297-298

If the Legislature repeals a law challenged by referendum petition under this part, the referendum petition is void and no further action on the referendum petition is required.

(H.B. 75 (2020) enrolled text)

Utah Code 20A-7-311(1), lines 266-269

If, at the time during the counting period described in Subsection 20A-7-307(2), the lieutenant governor determines that, at that point in time, an adequate number of signatures are certified to comply with the signature requirements, the lieutenant governor shall: (a) issue an order temporarily staying the law from going into effect;

The stay stays in place "regardless of whether a future count falls below the signature threshold" (lines 272-273) (H.B. 75 (2020) enrolled text).

Utah Code 20A-7-206(3)(c), line 66

post the name and [precinct] voter identification number of each registered voter

(H.B. 75 (2020) enrolled text)

The timeline

  1. Jan. 2, 2020 H.B. 75 numbered and publicly distributed, about signer information only (H.B. 75 (2020) bill status page; H.B. 75 (2020) as introduced).
  2. Jan. 28, 2020 The Legislature passes H.B. 185, repealing S.B. 2001, the 2019 tax law a referendum had qualified against (Ballotpedia News, Jan. 29, 2020; Bill Tracker: S.B. 2001 and H.B. 185).
  3. March 5, 2020 House committee adopts the 1st Substitute, which adds the stay and the void rule, and recommends it 9–0–2 (H.B. 75 (2020) bill status page; H.B. 75 (2020) 1st Substitute (Thurston); House Government Operations, favorable vote, March 5, 2020).
  4. March 9, 2020 House passes it 71–0–4 (House roll call, March 9, 2020).
  5. March 12, 2020 Senate passes it 27–0–2 under suspension (Senate roll call, March 12, 2020).
  6. March 28, 2020 Gov. Gary Herbert signs it (H.B. 75 (2020) bill status page).
  7. May 12, 2020 Takes effect (H.B. 75 (2020) bill status page).
  8. Dec. 2025 The Legislature repeals H.B. 267 in H.B. 2001; Ballotpedia reports a pending referendum is invalidated when the Legislature repeals the targeted law (Ballotpedia News, Dec. 11, 2025; Bill Tracker: H.B. 2001 (2025)).

What happened next

  • The rule took effect May 12, 2020 (H.B. 75 (2020) bill status page).
  • In December 2025, the Legislature repealed H.B. 267, the public-sector union law, after a referendum against it had qualified. Ballotpedia: "a pending veto referendum is automatically invalidated if the state legislature repeals the targeted legislation" (Ballotpedia News, Dec. 11, 2025; Bill Tracker: H.B. 2001 (2025)).
  • The Hive graded both repeals that ended a qualified referendum (H.B. 185 in 2020 and H.B. 2001 in 2025) an F (Bill Tracker: S.B. 2001 and H.B. 185; Bill Tracker: H.B. 2001 (2025); Ten Years of Petition Laws, 2016–2026 (Bill Tracker)).

Where it fits

How this law builds on the ones before it, from How they stack.

Repeal ends the referendum

  1. 2020 H.B. 185 F
    repealed the food-tax law the day its referendum qualified (about 152,000 signatures)
  2. 2020 H.B. 75 C−
    wrote it into law: a referendum petition "is void if the Legislature repeals the proposed law"; also added a temporary stay
  3. 2025 H.B. 2001 F
    repealed the union law; the referendum with more than 320,000 signatures ended

Where it ended up: Twice, referendums that had qualified ended without a vote. Each repeal got an F. The rule in between, H.B. 75, gets a Power F for the same reason; it lands at C− overall because it also added a temporary stay.
