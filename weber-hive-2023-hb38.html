Ten Days' Notice and Bound Packets

H.B. 38 rewrote the initiative and referendum law "to create consistency," gave lawmakers 10 days' notice of initiative hearings, and spelled out how petition packets are bound.

Posted Oct. 19, 2026 · Updated Oct. 19, 2026

Part of Ten Years of Petition Laws, 2016–2026: 35 changes to Utah's initiative and referendum rules, every one written and passed by the Legislature. H.B. 38 (2023) is one of them.

Short version

  • H.B. 38 (2023), primary sponsor Rep. Calvin R. Musselman, Senate sponsor Sen. David G. Buxton, amended 77 sections of law on initiatives and referendums (H.B. 38 (2023) enrolled text).
  • Its summary says it "revises provisions relating to initiatives and referenda to create consistency" and clarifies "who is responsible for certain requirements" (H.B. 38 (2023) enrolled text).
  • Sponsors of a statewide initiative must now give written notice of each public hearing at least 10 calendar days ahead, up from three (H.B. 38 (2023) enrolled text).
  • It spells out how initiative packets are bound, and a clerk may not certify a signature on a packet that was not verified (H.B. 38 (2023) enrolled text).
  • It passed both chambers with no votes against and was signed March 14, 2023 (Bill page and status (Chapter 107); Senate roll call, Feb. 27, 2023; House concurrence roll call, Feb. 28, 2023).

What the bill says

Quoted from the enrolled bill (H.B. 38 (2023) enrolled text). Text in [brackets] was struck.

Highlighted provisions (lines 13–15)

amends provisions relating to initiatives and referenda to clarify provisions and requirements, to clarify who is responsible for certain requirements, and to use consistent terms;

(H.B. 38 (2023) enrolled text)

Utah Code 20A-7-204.1(2) (line 681)

(a) before 5 p.m. at least [three] 10 calendar days before the date of the public hearing,

Sponsors' written notice of each hearing goes to the Lieutenant Governor and to the state lawmakers and county council or commission members from the region (H.B. 38 (2023) enrolled text).

Utah Code 20A-7-204(5) (lines 633–634)

The sponsors or an agent of the sponsors shall create the initiative packets by binding a copy of the initiative petition with the text of the proposed law

The text goes on: no more than 50 signature sheets, bound "together at the top"; packets do not need the same number of sheets (H.B. 38 (2023) enrolled text).

Utah Code 20A-7-206(5) (lines 855–857)

The county clerk may not certify a signature under Subsection (3): (a) on an initiative packet that is not verified in accordance with Section 20A-7-205;

(H.B. 38 (2023) enrolled text)

Utah Code 20A-7-203(6) (lines 589–590)

If the forms described in this section are substantially followed, the initiative petitions are sufficient, notwithstanding clerical and merely technical errors.

This rule for petition forms appears in the same bill (H.B. 38 (2023) enrolled text).

In their own words

Quoted exactly from the source named. A quote does not tell you everything a person thinks; it is what the record shows they said.

The 10-day hearing notice is a real, if minor, transparency gain. But its packet-binding formalization is the same rule that, per the Summit County case in the companion piece, threw out a majority of otherwise-valid signatures over a formatting issue unrelated to whether the signers were real, registered voters.

— The Weber County Hive, published grade reason, The Rules Keep Changing, Part 2 (Weber County Hive: The Rules Keep Changing, Part 2 (rubric))

What happened next

  • The 2023 code, amended by both H.B. 38 and S.B. 43, has sponsors give notice "before 5 p.m. at least 10 calendar days before the date of the public hearing" (Utah Code 20A-7-204.1, 2023 copy (Justia)).
  • The Hive's referendum docket reports that Summit County's 2025 Kimball Junction referendum had more than 6,000 signatures in 77 packets, but only about 3,214 were certified after packets were disqualified over binding rules; 4,554 were needed (Weber County Hive: Two Referendums, Zero Votes).
  • H.B. 38 is one of the 35 petition laws graded on the Ten Years of Petition Laws page (Weber County Hive: Ten Years of Petition Laws).
