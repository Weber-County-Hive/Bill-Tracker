A Gas Tax Cut, and Refinery Numbers Sealed Off

H.B. 575 cut the state gas tax for six months. The same bill makes refiners report their output to the state, and makes those reports secret from the public and the courts.

Posted Oct. 19, 2026 · Updated Oct. 19, 2026

Short version

  • H.B. 575 (2026), "Fuel Tax and Supply Amendments," has primary sponsor Rep. Calvin Roberts and Senate sponsor Sen. Brady Brammer (H.B. 575 (2026) enrolled text).
  • From July 1 to Dec. 31, 2026, the state gas tax is a flat $0.319 a gallon. The cut ends Jan. 1, 2027 (H.B. 575 (2026) enrolled text).
  • Starting July 1, 2026, refiners must report their output to the Office of Energy Development every quarter (H.B. 575 (2026) enrolled text).
  • The bill makes those reports a protected record, confidential commercial information and a trade secret. Courts may not order them disclosed, and knowingly disclosing them is a class A misdemeanor (H.B. 575 (2026) enrolled text).
  • It also sets a 120-day deadline for state environmental permits on pipelines and other "midstream" projects, and moves $11,903,900 from the General Fund to local B and C roads (H.B. 575 (2026) enrolled text).
  • A Senate committee first voted it down, 1–2, on March 2. Two days later it passed the committee 6–0 and the full Senate 24–1–4 the same day (LegiScan, H.B. 575 (2026): history and roll calls; Senate Revenue and Taxation Committee minutes, March 2, 2026).

What the bill says

Quoted from the enrolled bill (H.B. 575 (2026) enrolled text).

Utah Code 59-13-201(1)(a)(ii)

the commission shall apply a tax rate of $0.319 per gallon upon all motor fuel.

This applies "Beginning on July 1, 2026, and ending on December 31, 2026" (H.B. 575 (2026) enrolled text).

Utah Code 79-6-410(2)

Beginning July 1, 2026, and quarterly thereafter, a refiner shall provide to the office a

The "office" is the Office of Energy Development (H.B. 575 (2026) enrolled text).

Utah Code 79-6-410(4)(a)

a protected record under Title 63G, Chapter 2, Government Records Access and Management Act;

The same subsection also makes it "confidential commercial information" and "a trade secret for purposes of state law" (H.B. 575 (2026) enrolled text).

Utah Code 79-6-410(9)(b)

A court may not order disclosure of information reported under this section.

Subsection (9)(a) also says it "is not subject to subpoena, discovery, or admission into evidence" (H.B. 575 (2026) enrolled text).

Utah Code 79-6-410(6)(b)

may not be used as the basis for:

The list: regulatory enforcement, administrative action, market intervention, price regulation, civil or criminal investigation, or any action against a reporting entity except to enforce the reporting rule (H.B. 575 (2026) enrolled text).

Utah Code 79-6-410(12)(a)

A person who knowingly discloses information protected under this section is guilty of a class A misdemeanor.

A refiner may also sue a person or government entity that unlawfully discloses it (H.B. 575 (2026) enrolled text).

Utah Code 19-14-202

shall approve or deny an application within 120 days of the date on which the applicable division director

Extensions: up to 30 days for extraordinary circumstances, or longer if the applicant agrees (H.B. 575 (2026) enrolled text).

Utah Code 72-5-502

the department shall cooperate with and assist the pipeline proponent in negotiating and siting of

Applies to pipelines that cross state or federal highways (H.B. 575 (2026) enrolled text).

The timeline

  1. 2015 H.B. 362 adds a tax tied to the wholesale price of fuel to Utah's gas tax (Transportation Investment Advocacy Center, H.B. 362 (2015)).
  2. Feb. 16, 2026 H.B. 575 numbered and made public (LegiScan, H.B. 575 (2026): history and roll calls).
  3. Feb. 18, 2026 House Revenue and Taxation adopts the 1st Substitute and recommends it, 10–0–1 (LegiScan, H.B. 575 (2026): history and roll calls). ABC4 reports the fuel-export tax idea was dropped (ABC4, Feb. 18, 2026).
  4. Feb. 24, 2026 House passes it 67–2–6 (LegiScan, H.B. 575 (2026): history and roll calls).
  5. March 2, 2026 Senate committee motion fails 1–2–4 (Senate Revenue and Taxation Committee minutes, March 2, 2026).
  6. March 4, 2026 Senate committee recommends it 6–0–1; the full Senate passes it 24–1–4 the same day (LegiScan, H.B. 575 (2026): history and roll calls; Deseret News, March 4, 2026).
  7. March 23, 2026 Governor signs it (LegiScan, H.B. 575 (2026): history and roll calls).
  8. July 1, 2026 Flat $0.319 gas tax starts; first refinery reports due quarterly (H.B. 575 (2026) enrolled text).
  9. Jan. 1, 2027 The temporary rate is repealed (H.B. 575 (2026) enrolled text).

What happened next

  • The law took effect in May 2026; Utah Public Radio reported the cut runs July 1 through the end of 2026 (Utah Public Radio, May 7, 2026).
  • Rep. Roberts said Chevron, Maverik and Marathon committed to raising production over five years, Utah Public Radio reported (Utah Public Radio, May 7, 2026).
  • Utah Public Radio wrote that the final bill "passed unanimously." The roll calls show two House no votes and one Senate no vote (LegiScan, H.B. 575 (2026): history and roll calls; Utah Public Radio, May 7, 2026).
