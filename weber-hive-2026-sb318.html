No Secret Deals with Data Centers

S.B. 318 would have barred state agencies, cities, towns and counties from signing non-disclosure agreements with prospective data centers. It was introduced Feb. 23, 2026, never sent to a committee, and closed out at 11:59 p.m. on March 6.

Short version

  • S.B. 318 (2026), "Data Center Non Disclosure Amendments," primary sponsor Sen. Nate Blouin, would have barred state agencies, cities, towns and counties from entering non-disclosure agreements with prospective data centers (S.B. 318 as introduced).
  • A "data center" meant one with 20 megawatts or more of connected electrical load (S.B. 318 as introduced).
  • Any such agreement would be void. It would have to be posted on the government's website within 30 days and kept there at least five years. Residents could sue in district court: any Utah resident over a state agency's agreement, or a resident of the city or county over a local one (S.B. 318 as introduced).
  • It was introduced Feb. 23, 2026 and stayed in the Senate Rules Committee. It was never sent to a committee and never had a vote (S.B. 318 bill data record (actions and times), le.utah.gov; LegiScan, S.B. 318 (2026)).
  • At 11:59 p.m. on March 6, 2026, its enacting clause was struck by the Senate Secretary, the way every unfinished bill is closed out that night (S.B. 318 bill data record (actions and times), le.utah.gov; The Weber County Hive, Bills That Did the Opposite, Part 4 (11:59 p.m., March 6)).

What the bill says

Quoted from the introduced bill, new Utah Code 63G-2-109 (state agencies). Sections 10-8-95 and 17-60-509 apply the same rules to cities, towns and counties (S.B. 318 as introduced).

Utah Code 63G-2-109(2)(a)

may not enter into a non-disclosure agreement with a prospective data center.

Applies to agreements entered on or after the effective date, May 6, 2026 (S.B. 318 as introduced).

Utah Code 63G-2-109(1)(b)(ii)

has a connected electrical load capacity of 20 megawatts or greater.

Part of the definition of a data center (S.B. 318 as introduced).

Utah Code 63G-2-109(3)(a)

A non-disclosure agreement that violates Subsection (2) is void and unenforceable.

The rest of the contract stays in force (S.B. 318 as introduced).

Utah Code 63G-2-109(4)

A state agency shall publicly disclose any contract or agreement that violates Subsection (2)

Posted within 30 days and kept at least five years (S.B. 318 as introduced).

Utah Code 63G-2-109(5)

A resident of this state may bring an action in district court to:

Enforcement by any resident (S.B. 318 as introduced).
