The Golf Water Report That Never Came

H.B. 188 would have made golf courses report their yearly water use to the state and post it online. A House committee held it, and it died.

Posted Oct. 19, 2026 · Updated Oct. 19, 2026

Short version

  • H.B. 188 (2023), primary sponsor Rep. Douglas R. Welton, would have required golf courses and driving ranges to report their yearly water use to the Division of Water Resources and post it online (H.B. 188 (2023) bill status page, le.utah.gov; H.B. 188 as introduced).
  • The 1st Substitute added a Utah State University study, a grant program of up to $30 million for water-saving upgrades, and a six-member Golf Advisory Board. It moved the reporting start to July 1, 2028 (1st Substitute H.B. 188).
  • Two people testified on Jan. 25, 2023. Both spoke against it. No one spoke for it (House Natural Resources, Agriculture, and Environment minutes, Jan. 25, 2023).
  • On Feb. 10, 2023, the committee adopted the 1st Substitute 11–0–3, then voted 9–2–3 to hold it (House Natural Resources, Agriculture, and Environment minutes, Feb. 10, 2023).
  • The House struck the enacting clause and filed the bill on March 3, 2023. It never reached the House floor or the Senate (H.B. 188 (2023) bill status page, le.utah.gov).

What the bill says

Quoted from the 1st Substitute, the version the committee held (1st Substitute H.B. 188).

Proposed Utah Code 73-1-22(3)(c)

beginning July 1, 2028, an owner or operator of a golf course or driving range shall report to the division

(1st Substitute H.B. 188)

Proposed Utah Code 73-1-22(3)(d)

The division shall publish the information reported under Subsection (3)(c)

(1st Substitute H.B. 188)

Proposed Utah Code 73-1-22(2)(a)

Utah State University shall study the use of water on golf courses or driving ranges in the state.

Findings due by June 30, 2026 (1st Substitute H.B. 188).

Proposed Utah Code 73-1-22(4)(a)

up to an aggregate total of $30,000,000, the division may issue a grant

(1st Substitute H.B. 188)

What happened next

  • The bill did not pass. No golf water reporting rule from H.B. 188 is in Utah law (H.B. 188 (2023) bill status page, le.utah.gov).
  • The USU study due June 30, 2026, the 2028 reporting rule, the grant program and the advisory board were never created (H.B. 188 (2023) bill status page, le.utah.gov; 1st Substitute H.B. 188).
