Repealed Before the Vote: The Union Referendum

Voters were set to decide H.B. 267 on Nov. 3, 2026. The same two sponsors repealed it in a one-day special session, and the referendum ended.

Posted Oct. 19, 2026 · Updated Oct. 19, 2026

Part of Ten Years of Petition Laws, 2016–2026: 35 changes to Utah's initiative and referendum rules, every one written and passed by the Legislature. H.B. 2001 (2025) is one of them.

Short version

  • H.B. 267 (2025), sponsored by Rep. Jordan Teuscher and Sen. Kirk Cullimore, banned collective bargaining for public employees. It passed the House 42–32 and the Senate 16–13 and was signed Feb. 14, 2025 (Ballotpedia News, June 25, 2025; Deseret News, Feb. 14, 2025 (H.B. 267 signed)).
  • Citizens submitted about 320,000 signatures for a referendum; 251,590 were verified, far more than the 140,748 needed. It qualified for the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot (Deseret News, April 16, 2025; Deseret News, June 21, 2025 (certified)).
  • On Dec. 9, 2025, in a special session, the same two sponsors carried H.B. 2001, a two-section bill (a repealer and an effective date) that repealed H.B. 267. It passed 60–9–6 and 26–1–2 (H.B. 2001 enrolled text; House roll call, Dec. 9, 2025; Senate roll call, Dec. 9, 2025).
  • Under the 2020 rule from H.B. 75, a referendum petition "is void" when the Legislature repeals the law, so the vote was cancelled (H.B. 75 (2020) enrolled text).
  • Grade: F, the same grade the Hive published for H.B. 185 (2020), which did the same thing to the food-tax referendum (Bill Tracker case file, S.B. 2001 and H.B. 185).

What the bill says

The whole bill is two sections (H.B. 2001 enrolled text).

Section 1 · lines 19–21

Section 1. Repealer. H.B. 267, Public Sector Labor Union Amendments, Laws of Utah, 2025 General Session is repealed.

(H.B. 2001 enrolled text)

Section 2 · lines 22–29

Section 2. Effective Date. This bill takes effect: (1) except as provided in Subsection (2), February 8, 2026; or (2) if approved by two-thirds of all members elected to each house: (a) upon approval by the governor;

Both chambers passed it by more than two-thirds (H.B. 2001 enrolled text; House roll call, Dec. 9, 2025; Senate roll call, Dec. 9, 2025).

H.B. 75 (2020) · Utah Code 20A-7-311 · lines 297–298

If the Legislature repeals a law challenged by referendum petition under this part, the referendum petition is void and no further action on the referendum petition is required.

The rule that ended the referendum (H.B. 75 (2020) enrolled text).

The timeline

  1. Feb. 14, 2025 Gov. Cox signs H.B. 267 (Deseret News, Feb. 14, 2025 (H.B. 267 signed)).
  2. April 16, 2025 Protect Utah Workers says it has over 320,000 signatures; 140,748 were needed (Deseret News, April 16, 2025).
  3. April 28, 2025 The Lt. Governor's office confirms the threshold is met (Salt Lake Tribune, April 28, 2025).
  4. May 6, 2025 Lt. Gov. Henderson issues an order staying the law from taking effect, using the stay power H.B. 75 created (FOX 13, May 2025 (stay order); H.B. 75 (2020) enrolled text).
  5. June 21, 2025 Referendum certified: 251,590 signatures verified; set for Nov. 3, 2026 (Deseret News, June 21, 2025 (certified); KUER, June 23, 2025).
  6. Dec. 7, 2025 Gov. Cox calls a special session; item 5 is the repeal of H.B. 267 (Governor's proclamation, Dec. 7, 2025; KSL, Dec. 7, 2025).
  7. Dec. 8, 2025 Legislative leaders and Protect Utah Workers announce the repeal (House release, Dec. 8, 2025 (Adams, Schultz and Protect Utah Workers)).
  8. Dec. 9, 2025 H.B. 2001 passes both chambers the same evening (House roll call, Dec. 9, 2025; Senate roll call, Dec. 9, 2025).
  9. Dec. 11, 2025 Gov. Cox signs it (Governor's release on signing, Dec. 11, 2025).

What happened next

  • The referendum came off the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot (Ballotpedia News, Dec. 11, 2025).
  • Rep. Teuscher wrote that the ideas "remain just as important today as they were when the bill passed" (Daily Utah Chronicle, Dec. 11, 2025).
  • This is the second time a qualified statewide referendum ended this way; the first was the 2020 food-tax referendum, ended by H.B. 185 (Bill Tracker case file, S.B. 2001 and H.B. 185).

Where it fits

How this law builds on the ones before it, from How they stack.

Repeal ends the referendum

  1. 2020 H.B. 185 F
    repealed the food-tax law the day its referendum qualified (about 152,000 signatures)
  2. 2020 H.B. 75 C−
    wrote it into law: a referendum petition "is void if the Legislature repeals the proposed law"; also added a temporary stay
  3. 2025 H.B. 2001 F
    repealed the union law; the referendum with more than 320,000 signatures ended

Where it ended up: Twice, referendums that had qualified ended without a vote. Each repeal got an F. The rule in between, H.B. 75, gets a Power F for the same reason; it lands at C− overall because it also added a temporary stay.
