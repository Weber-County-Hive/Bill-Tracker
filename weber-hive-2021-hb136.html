Badges and Hourly Pay for Gatherers

H.B. 136 banned paying initiative and referendum gatherers by the signature, required paid gatherers to wear a badge, and had gatherers show the law to signers before they sign.

Posted Oct. 19, 2026 · Updated Oct. 19, 2026

Part of Ten Years of Petition Laws, 2016–2026: 35 changes to Utah's initiative and referendum rules, every one written and passed by the Legislature. H.B. 136 (2021) is one of them.

Short version

  • H.B. 136 (2021), primary sponsor Rep. Jordan D. Teuscher, says no one may pay a person to gather initiative or referendum signatures "based on a rate per signature"; pay must be "solely on an hourly rate" (H.B. 136 (2021) enrolled text; H.B. 136 (2021) bill status page).
  • Paid gatherers must wear a badge with an ID number, the measure's title, the words "Paid Signature Gatherer" and who is paying them (H.B. 136 (2021) enrolled text).
  • The packet no longer carries a copy of the law. Instead a gatherer must show the signer a printed or digital copy and wait for them to read it (H.B. 136 (2021) enrolled text).
  • Initiative sponsors must email signers who give an email address. Breaking the gatherer rules is a class B misdemeanor (H.B. 136 (2021) enrolled text).
  • It passed the House 42–30–3, the Senate 22–7–0 and the House again 42–22–11, and was signed March 23, 2021 (H.B. 136 (2021) bill status page).

What the bill says

Quoted from the enrolled bill (H.B. 136 (2021) enrolled text).

Utah Code 20A-7-104(1)-(2), lines 103-107

A person may not pay a person to gather signatures under this chapter based on a rate per signature, on a rate per verified signature, or on the initiative or referendum qualifying for the ballot. (2) A person that pays a person to gather signatures under this section shall base the payment solely on an hourly rate.

(H.B. 136 (2021) enrolled text)

Utah Code 20A-7-104(4), lines 109-112

An individual who is paid to gather signatures for a petition described in this chapter shall, while gathering signatures, wear a badge on the front of the individual's torso

The badge shows an ID number, the measure's title, "Paid Signature Gatherer" and the paying entity's name (lines 116-122) (H.B. 136 (2021) enrolled text).

Utah Code 20A-7-104(6)(a), lines 136-138

shall, before collecting a signature from an individual, present to the individual a printed or digital copy of the initiative or referendum and wait for the individual to read the initiative or referendum;

(H.B. 136 (2021) enrolled text)

Utah Code 20A-7-104(7), line 142

A person who violates this section is guilty of a class B misdemeanor.

(H.B. 136 (2021) enrolled text)

The timeline

  1. Jan. 12, 2021 H.B. 136 numbered and publicly distributed (H.B. 136 (2021) bill status page).
  2. Feb. 2, 2021 House committee adopts the 1st Substitute and recommends it 8–3 (H.B. 136 (2021) bill status page; Deseret News, Feb. 5, 2021).
  3. Feb. 5, 2021 Deseret News reports supporters and opponents, including two local referendum organizers from Provo (Deseret News, Feb. 5, 2021).
  4. Feb. 8, 2021 A House floor amendment fails; the House passes the bill 42–30–3 (H.B. 136 (2021) bill status page).
  5. March 1, 2021 Senate committee adopts the 2nd Substitute and recommends it 5–2–1 (H.B. 136 (2021) bill status page).
  6. March 4–5, 2021 Senate passes it 22–7–0; House concurs 42–22–11 (H.B. 136 (2021) bill status page).
  7. March 23, 2021 Gov. Spencer Cox signs it (H.B. 136 (2021) bill status page).
  8. May 5, 2021 Takes effect (H.B. 136 (2021) bill status page).
  9. May 10, 2021 KUTV reports a federal lawsuit naming Gov. Cox and Lt. Gov. Henderson, arguing the law burdens gatherers' anonymous political speech (KUTV, May 10, 2021).

What happened next

  • The rules took effect May 5, 2021 (H.B. 136 (2021) bill status page).
  • In May 2021, a federal lawsuit challenged parts of the law; Gov. Cox and Lt. Gov. Henderson declined to comment (KUTV, May 10, 2021). We did not find the outcome.
  • In 2026, H.B. 32 kept hourly-only pay for initiative and referendum gatherers and added required training (H.B. 32 (2026) enrolled text; Ten Years of Petition Laws, 2016–2026 (Bill Tracker)).

  4. 2026 H.B. 32 C+
    online training before gathering; packets from untrained gatherers rejected; kept hourly-only pay

Where it ended up: Each round added rules for gatherers except 2024, which removed one.
