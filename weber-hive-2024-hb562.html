Eight Days to Build a Stadium District

H.B. 562 created the Utah Fairpark Area Investment and Restoration District. It can put up to $900 million toward a baseball stadium. It was made public on Feb. 20, 2024 and passed both houses by Feb. 28.

Posted Oct. 19, 2026 · Updated Oct. 19, 2026

Short version

  • H.B. 562 (2024) has primary sponsor Rep. Ryan D. Wilcox and Senate floor sponsor Sen. Lincoln Fillmore, with 16 cosponsors (Utah Legislature bill page, H.B. 562 (2024)).
  • It created the Utah Fairpark Area Investment and Restoration District around the State Fairpark in Salt Lake City (H.B. 562 2nd Substitute text).
  • The district can issue bonds. Its money for a stadium "may not exceed $900,000,000" (H.B. 562 2nd Substitute text).
  • The fiscal note says the state gives up $52.4 million in sales tax during stadium construction, and Salt Lake City and County give up $13.5 million (Fiscal note, 2nd Substitute).
  • It also raises a statewide car rental tax, about $6.05 million a year once a team signs a franchise agreement (H.B. 562 2nd Substitute text; Fiscal note, 2nd Substitute).
  • It was made public Feb. 20, 2024. The House passed it Feb. 27, 51–21–3. The Senate passed it the next day, 25–4, without a Senate committee hearing (Utah Legislature bill page, H.B. 562 (2024)).

The timeline

  1. Feb. 20, 2024 H.B. 562 numbered and made public (Utah Legislature bill page, H.B. 562 (2024)).
  2. Feb. 23, 2024 House Government Operations hears it: 12 speakers for, 5 against (mostly hotel owners), 2 neutral. Recommended 8–2–3 (House Government Operations Committee minutes, Feb. 23, 2024).
  3. Feb. 26, 2024 Circled on the House floor (Utah Legislature bill page, H.B. 562 (2024)).
  4. Feb. 27, 2024 House adopts the 2nd Substitute by voice vote and passes it 51–21–3 (Utah Legislature bill page, H.B. 562 (2024)).
  5. Feb. 27, 2024, evening Senate puts it on the calendar the same day; the 2nd Substitute fiscal note becomes public (Utah Legislature bill page, H.B. 562 (2024)).
  6. Feb. 28, 2024 Senate passes it 25–4 (Utah Legislature bill page, H.B. 562 (2024)).
  7. March 19, 2024 Governor signs it (Utah Legislature bill page, H.B. 562 (2024)).

What happened next

  • The stadium money depends on Utah getting a Major League Baseball team by 2032, the Senate news release said (Utah Senate news release, Feb. 28, 2024).
  • Later bills changed the rules around the district: S.B. 197 (2026) rewrote how state sales tax growth is shared among UFAIR and similar districts (Bill Tracker: S.B. 197 (2026) (Hive)).
